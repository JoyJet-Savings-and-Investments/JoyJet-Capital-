          About

          JoyJet Capital Unlimited

          Where Your Money Takes Flight

          This web app helps you track cash, holdings, goals, and trade history on your device with clarity.

          What this app provides

          • On-device ledger: Deposits, buys, sells, withdrawals recorded locally with FIFO cost basis.
          • Multi-asset book: Crypto, stocks, and ETF symbols with charts and allocation.
          • Savings goal: $10,000 by Dec 31, 2026 with countdown and contribution hints.
          • Blueprints: Allocation mixes with preview before confirm.
          • Exports: Ledger CSV, FIFO tax CSV (with long/short), JSON backup, printable statement download.
          • Support: Email and WhatsApp for human help and plan questions.
